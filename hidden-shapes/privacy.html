Hidden Shapes: Tangram Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data we collect

Hidden Shapes does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location, and we do not run servers that receive your personal data.

Your progress (solved figures) and your sound setting are stored only on your device (local storage). Uninstalling the app deletes them.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen, an occasional full-screen ad between puzzles, and an optional rewarded ad you can choose to watch for a hint.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and interactions with ads, under Google's Partner Sites policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Collection → Privacy inside the game. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

3. Children

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Rewarded ads start only when you tap “Watch ad”.
