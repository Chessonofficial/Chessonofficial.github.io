Pop Chess — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Publisher: Chesson

1. Data we collect

Pop Chess does not require an account. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location, and we do not run our own servers that store personal data. Your progress (levels, coins, themes and settings) is stored locally on your device.

2. Ads (Android and iOS)

The mobile apps show ads through Google AdMob. Ads are requested as child-directed and limited to general-audience (G-rated) content. AdMob may process device identifiers, approximate location derived from the IP address and ad interactions to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, under Google's partner policies.

3. Analytics

On Android and iOS we use Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous gameplay statistics (for example which levels are played and completed). This data is not used to identify you. Details: Firebase Privacy.

4. In-app purchases

Optional purchases (coin packs, remove ads) are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.

5. Facebook and Messenger (Instant Games) version

The Facebook/Messenger version runs in Meta's Zero Permissions mode: it does not access your name, profile picture, friends or email. Your progress is saved in your browser and in Meta's game-scoped cloud storage (linked to a game-specific player ID). Anonymous gameplay events are sent to Meta's Instant Games analytics. Ads are served by Meta Audience Network under the Meta Privacy Policy. This version has no in-app purchases.

6. Data deletion

We keep no personal data on our servers. To delete local progress, uninstall the app or clear your browser data. To delete your Facebook game data, remove Pop Chess under Facebook Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites → Instant Games. You can also email chessonofficial@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request"; we respond within 30 days.

7. Children

Pop Chess is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and ads in the mobile apps are restricted to child-safe content.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: chessonofficial@gmail.com · Support: popchessapp@gmail.com
